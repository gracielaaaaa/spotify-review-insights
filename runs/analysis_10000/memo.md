## Recommendation

Focus next quarter on usability, specifically the ad load issue: "Too many ads interrupt listening experience" has 2,842 complaints [C005] and a priority score of 5,892 [C008]. It is the largest classified, specific issue. The premium paywall (billing) should be scoped alongside it, since both concern the free-tier experience.

The generic "General dissatisfaction" bucket ranks first at 48,110 [C004], but it is non-specific venting (for example, review 1a43d900-7a6f-4952-b338-b1c7fb58e312) and cannot be acted on, so I exclude it.

## Evidence

- Ads: 2,842 complaints [C005], mean severity 2.073188 [C007]. Users describe ads after each song (review cf7cb282-1a38-4a5f-a733-c41bf2926117) and frequent interruptions (review fa5eba46-75b8-4aba-8efd-a0e47c186906).
- Usability roll-up figures: 3,467 complaints, severity sum 7,546, across 6 issues.
- Secondary usability items: shuffle controls at 231 complaints [C021] and priority 634 [C024]; this is a smaller, discrete fix (review c747a3ba-29ec-4188-b5c3-38d662a84ae6).

## Alternatives considered

- **Billing/support:** the premium paywall has 854 complaints [C009] and priority 2,515 [C012], with higher mean severity 2.944965 [C011]. It is a strong second and is linked to ads strategically (review 88cc8f74-0622-4064-b652-894fe63e1fd6). Roll-up: billing 1,094 complaints, severity sum 3,120. Support alone has only 9 complaints (roll-up).
- **Playback:** crashes have 216 complaints [C013], priority 786 [C016] and high mean severity 3.638889 [C015] (review 647975bc-5143-4c8f-a38e-48b4acfe2916). Roll-up: 890 complaints, severity sum 2,807. It ranks lower on volume but is the best candidate if reliability is the goal.
- **Access:** login failure has 152 complaints [C029] with the highest severity, 3.907895 [C031], and priority 594 [C032] (review 9d0cf6e4-f98d-46e7-a606-efeb713dbd47). Roll-up: access 183 complaints. Low volume, but a cheap, severe fix.

## Risks and limitations

Coverage: only about 19.98% of source rows (131,976 of 660,622) were classified; 528,646 were quarantined and unclassified rows are excluded from all counts. Reviews are self-selected public app-store text from May 2022 to Nov 2023, so counts are not prevalence. Severity is a model-assigned score. Ads and paywall are likely business-model tradeoffs, not just defects, and quotes with uninstall language show stated intent, not observed churn. No revenue impact is measured.

## Next steps

1. Have product and monetization review whether ad frequency or placement can change without hurting revenue.
2. Scope the paywall messaging and gating of basic features jointly.
3. Run a small parallel fix for login failures and crashes given their severity.
4. Validate the classification on a sample of the unclassified rows to check for bias.
