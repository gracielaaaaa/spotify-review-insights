## Recommendation

Put next quarter's effort into usability, starting with free-tier ad frequency. It is the largest specific issue in the classified data, with 850 complaints [C005] and a priority score of 1,713 [C008]. Mean severity is 2.015294 [C007], so it is high-volume and moderate-pain. The only larger bucket, general negative sentiment (8,200 complaints [C001]), is not actionable as a product issue.

## Evidence

Coverage figures: 66,577 of 660,622 source rows were classified (about 10.08%). Unclassified rows are excluded from all counts. Roll-up figures: usability has 879 complaints across 6 issues, and ads account for nearly all of them.

Ad complaints describe interruption frequency, for example "10 minutes of music and 4 of ads, this is ridiculous" (review 3bfefa06-133c-419b-a435-1fea919d6a44) and "1 minute of add after every 2 songs" (review 7f057d48-0855-4325-950b-44f9e6fc2ee1).

Other usability items are much smaller: shuffle controls have 12 complaints [C017] and a priority score of 33 [C020]. The redesigned layout has 9 complaints [C029] and a score of 24 [C032].

## Alternatives considered

- **Billing (premium paywall):** 45 complaints [C009], score 131 [C012], mean severity 2.911111 [C011]. It ranks second by score and is closely related to ads, since both concern free-tier limits. Reviews describe features moving behind premium (review 21915fcd-9a35-48ba-b277-6296c4154603). It could be paired with the ads work.
- **Playback:** App crashes after updates have 12 complaints [C013], score 46 [C016], and mean severity 3.833333 [C015]. Volume is low but severity is high, so this is the best candidate for a parallel stability fix (review 55cc3abf-c295-4591-8c3a-78085cfdefaa).
- **Access:** Login failure has 7 complaints [C021] with the highest mean severity, 4.000000 [C023], and a score of 28 [C024]. It is too small to lead, but one review states intent to unsubscribe (review 686fa8a8-f220-4ddd-b283-d61dd242051a). That is stated intent, not observed churn.

## Risks and limitations

Only about 10.08% of rows were classified (coverage figure), and unclassified rows are excluded, so counts may not represent all reviews. Reviews are self-selected public app-store text from May 2022 to Nov 2023. Complaint volume is not revenue or churn impact. Ad complaints may reflect free users who would not be affected by premium-side changes. The priority score multiplies volume by severity, which favors common moderate issues over rare severe ones. The general-criticism bucket is large but undiagnosed.

## Next steps

1. Have product and ads teams review ad frequency and pacing for free users, and define a test.
2. Assess whether the paywall and ad issues can be addressed together.
3. Assign a small engineering effort to update-related crashes and login failures.
4. Expand classification coverage and re-run the ranking before committing the full quarter.
