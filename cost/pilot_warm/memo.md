## Recommendation

Put next quarter's effort on billing, starting with the premium paywall issue: basic playback features locked behind premium. It has the highest priority score, 29 [C004], from 10 complaints [C001] at mean severity 2.9 [C003]. Treat the crash/won't-open issue as the second focus.

## Evidence

- Premium paywall: 10 complaints [C001], severity sum 29 [C002]. Reviewers describe losing the ability to skip or choose a song without a subscription (review a3605554-5c3a-441d-836e-e9fdea83eb2e) and being unable to pick a song (review d146397f-4a94-4980-be00-ae9212eca3a7).
- Crashes after updates: 3 complaints [C013], mean severity 4.0 [C015], priority score 12 [C016]. One reviewer says the latest update made the app crash and won't open (review 55cc3abf-c295-4591-8c3a-78085cfdefaa).

## Alternatives considered

Its top issue is ads on the free tier: 6 complaints [C009], priority score 15 [C012] (review c2738a4f-e57c-46bc-a4ad-8ec5a4b5d5cd). Crashes score 12 [C016] and stops/skips score 7 [C020] (review 6c257eba-3dca-49f6-b7f0-4c4484d81e26). No single playback issue matches the paywall score, though crashes are the most severe at 4.0 [C015]. Playback could be second if leadership weights severity over volume.
- Access: roll-up of only 2 complaints with severity sum 7 (roll-up figures). Password reset has 1 complaint [C033] at severity 4.0 [C035]. Volume is too low to prioritize.
- General app dissatisfaction: 10 complaints [C005], score 20 [C008]. It is vague and not actionable, so it is not recommended as a focus.

## Risks and limitations

Coverage: 100 of 100 in-scope reviews were classified (100% coverage figure), so no rows were excluded as unclassified. The sample is small and self-selected public app-store text from May 2022 to Nov 2023, so counts are not prevalence. Severity is model-assigned. Some reviewers say they are leaving or uninstalling, but that is stated intent, not observed churn. The paywall complaints may partly reflect a specific update, which this data cannot confirm. The roll-up shows playback with a higher severity sum than billing (32 vs 29), so the ranking depends on grouping choices.

- Check telemetry and crash reports for the crash/won't-open issue after recent releases.
- Validate findings on a larger, dated review sample before committing the full quarter.
