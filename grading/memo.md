## Recommendation

Focus next quarter on billing, led by the premium paywall on basic features (priority score 21,636 [C008]). The ads issue (a usability item, 18,856 [C012]) is closely tied to the same free-versus-premium tension and should be scoped together with it. The largest single item, general criticism (78,558 [C004]), is vague and not actionable on its own.

## Evidence

- Premium paywall: 7,370 complaints [C005], mean severity 2.935685 [C007]. Reviewers say non-premium users cannot play chosen songs or skip (review 30e0815b-0183-464a-a2da-e47e6a52b9d3; review e74d7b4e-fbf8-4a56-97fa-8b03500a3eae).
- Ads: 8,509 complaints [C009], mean severity 2.216007 [C011]. Users describe back-to-back ad streaks (review c6e8a207-c0c0-43eb-8c75-48345a8838c4).
- Roll-up figures: billing has 9,072 complaints and 26,371 severity sum across 6 issues.

## Alternatives considered

- Playback (roll-up: 7,125 complaints, 22,637 severity sum, highest mean severity of the big topics at 3.18). Crashes or failure to open: 1,794 complaints [C013], severity 3.642140 [C015], priority 6,534 [C016] (review d3cbe287-e147-49ac-a396-ac220bbe0cd5). Stops/skips: 1,790 complaints [C017], priority 5,731 [C020]. This is the best second choice, especially as a stability fix.
- Usability beyond ads (roll-up: 14,193 complaints, 34,183 severity sum across the topic). Shuffle controls: 2,003 complaints [C021], priority 5,581 [C024] (review b1f70c63-b271-480d-b353-a987d30e0bb8). Large in volume but lower severity.
- Access (roll-up: 1,410 complaints, 5,482 severity sum, mean severity 3.89). Login failure: 1,131 complaints [C025], severity 3.877100 [C027], priority 4,385 [C028] (review e6ca451b-404d-4739-9099-0ded17dd560c). Smallest volume, but the most severe per complaint, so it could be a targeted fix.
- Support has only 76 complaints in the roll-up, so it ranks lowest.

## Risks and limitations

Coverage figures: 237,939 of 660,622 source rows were completed (36.02% of rows); quarantined rows (422,683) are unclassified and excluded from all counts. Reviews are self-selected public app-store text from May 2022 to Nov 2023, so they do not represent all users. Severity is a model-assigned score. Cancellation language is stated intent, not observed churn. Counts show complaint volume, not revenue impact. Paywall and ad complaints may reflect intended business model design rather than defects, so product changes carry commercial tradeoffs.

2. Scope ad-frequency options alongside the paywall work.
3. Assign a stability team to crash and open failures in parallel.
4. Check the login-failure complaints for a quick fix.
5. Re-run the analysis with fuller classification coverage before committing.
